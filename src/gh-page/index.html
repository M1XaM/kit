Launch Kit

Already installed? This opens your local Kit instance. Tick “Always allow” in the browser prompt and it will launch instantly every time — no more asking. First time here? Grab it from GitHub.

🤖 AI, Without the Cloud

Remove image backgrounds, summarize and paraphrase text with neural nets running on your hardware. Pick a model tier to match your machine, download weights on demand, and let it ride your NVIDIA GPU when one is around.

⚡ Heavier Than a Browser Allows

A high-performance Go engine on your OS does the heavy lifting, so gigabyte videos, massive PDFs and 200-image batch jobs sail past the browser memory limits that crash ordinary web tools.

🔒 100% Local & Private

Your files never leave your device — even OCR runs inside your browser. No uploads, no tracking, no cloud, no bandwidth limits.

🧰 One Dashboard, 50+ Tools

PDFs, video, audio, images, archives, screen recording, checksums, a Markdown + LaTeX notepad that saves every keystroke to plain text… searchable, favoritable, and honest about whether each tool runs in-browser or on the local engine.

🔗 Native App Experience

Bookmark kit://start and launch Kit like a real application. Close the tab and the engine quietly shuts itself down within seconds — no lingering background services.

📊 Knows Your Machine

Live CPU, RAM and GPU monitoring built into the dashboard and the AI tools — Kit recommends the model your hardware can actually handle and shows what ran where.
